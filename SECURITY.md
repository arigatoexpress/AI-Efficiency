# Security and Data Handling

This repository must stay safe for broad review.

## Do Not Commit

- API keys, tokens, passwords, cookies, private keys, or credentials.
- Real customer names, addresses, phone numbers, signatures, delivery photos, or
  proof-of-delivery material.
- Real package tracking numbers, route manifests, station-sensitive operating
  details, security procedures, or proprietary internal reports.
- Employee records, performance notes, HR material, or sensitive scheduling
  details.
- Live system URLs or private dashboards unless approved for sharing.

## AI Tool Use Rules

- Use approved tools and approved accounts for work material.
- Treat AI output as a draft until a human verifies it.
- Do not use AI to make employment, discipline, safety, legal, medical,
  financial, or customer-impacting decisions without the required review path.
- Do not automate customer or external communications without explicit human
  approval and governance review.

## Reporting a Concern

If sensitive material is accidentally committed:

1. Stop sharing the link.
2. Notify the repo owner immediately.
3. Remove the material in a new commit.
4. Rotate any exposed credential through the approved internal process.
5. Document what happened and what was changed.

Do not paste the secret or sensitive data into an issue.
