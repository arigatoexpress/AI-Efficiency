Why this exists

FedEx's enterprise AI Education & Literacy program asks every team to find safe, practical AI wins. This hub is our regional contribution: instead of scattered experiments, it's one coherent set of tools and resources that share the same principles, the same governance gate, and the same plain-English voice — so a manager can start today and a reviewer can trust what they see.

◆Start today

Copy-paste prompts and a runnable offline dashboard mean value in minutes, not a project plan.

◆Safe by design

Real data stays local; only public and synthetic data is ever shown or shared. Every output gets human review.

◆Small, measurable pilots

Each tool is a focused pilot with a clear success metric — not broad, vague automation.

1How it all connects

Ideas come in the front door and become projects. Daily prompts power managers' routine drafts. Before a shift, Station Ops Intelligence turns public risk signals into reviewed briefs; after the week, the Dock Efficiency Signal Lab flags which dock-KPI moves are real and the TLH/SPH Efficiency Explorer shows which lever moved them. Governance gates everything, and a shared data model is the future state.

Two halves of one ops console: Station Ops Intelligence looks outward before a shift; the Dock Efficiency Signal Lab looks inward after the week. Same principles, same governance gate, same plain-English voice.

2The tools

Five focused tools, each a small pilot with a clear job. Pick by the question you're trying to answer.

Station Ops Intelligence

Built · Live

A public-data console that surfaces weather, road, and seismic risk before a shift and drafts pre-shift / handoff / after-action memos with AI — every item labeled and "needs manager verification."

Use this when: you want to see external risk before a shift and get a draft brief you can edit.
▶ Live demo Project & quick-start

Dock Efficiency Signal Lab

Built · Offline

An offline, single-file dashboard that applies quality-control statistics (SPC), trading-style indicators, and forecasting to weekly dock KPIs — ranking top/bottom performers with a dollars-vs-plan view, scoped to your districts.

Use this when: you have weekly TLH / %-of-goal numbers and want to know which moves are real and which districts to focus on.
▶ Open the app (offline) How it works

TLH / SPH Efficiency Explorer

Built · Offline

An offline, single-file companion to the Signal Lab that splits each facility's week-over-week efficiency change into its two real levers — throughput (SPH) and labor hours (TLH) — so an hours-cut gain is never mistaken for a productivity win.

Use this when: efficiency moved and you need to know which lever moved it before you replicate it or escalate it.
▶ Open the app (offline) How it works

AI Idea Intake Agent

Concept

A safe Teams/Copilot or Gemini-channel flow to capture employee AI ideas, gather feedback, and triage use cases — with a scoring rubric and an intake template you can reuse today.

Use this when: you have an AI idea and want it captured, scored, and routed safely.
Intake template Triage rubric

Delivery Markets Lab

External reference

A synthetic, prediction-market-style prototype kept as a teaching reference — a governance example of how to make an ambitious external concept tangible without touching real data.

Use this when: you want a worked example of safely documenting an external AI-Studio prototype.

4Agentic AI

A chat answers; an agent acts. Our shared mental model is a four-rung agency ladder — the rung an output came from tells you how much human review it needs. Tools are the control surface: every tool an agent can call must be justified.

RungWhat it doesWhere this repo is
1 · AssistantAnswers and drafts in one turn — no tools, no steps.Live prompts & Gemini drafts
2 · Tool-using assistantReads a file, searches, or calculates when you ask.Live the offline apps
3 · Supervised agentPlans steps toward a goal, pauses for human sign-off.Planned intake agent, Teams-first
4 · Autonomous agentRuns a workflow end-to-end without a human in the loop.Gated not for FedEx ops data until governance approves
The safe habit: before you trust an output, ask which rung it came from. Full guide: Agentic AI for operations · engineering path: Google Cloud + ADK · first governed agent: intake agent roadmap.

The three principles, once

  • Protect people, customers, and the company first. Never paste confidential, customer, route, security, employee, or proprietary data into public or unapproved AI tools.
  • Keep real data local — public & synthetic only. Tools demo on synthetic data; real numbers stay on your machine.
  • Human review before any action. AI drafts and analyzes; a person decides, sends, and is accountable.
Planned / research tracks — Foundry shared ontologyTeams & Telegram intakePrivacy (FHE) research — are on the roadmap, clearly marked as future state.
