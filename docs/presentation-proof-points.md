# Presentation Proof Points

Use these public, official sources when a leader asks why this work matters, how
it aligns with FedEx direction, or where a specific claim came from.

## How to use this page

- Keep it open in a backup browser tab during the presentation.
- Use it for Q&A, not as the main demo.
- Quote sparingly. Paraphrase the strategic point and point people to the
  source.

## Presenter Framing

Use this phrasing when you want to stay aligned and avoid overclaiming:

> "This repo is not claiming to be an official FedEx product. It is a regional,
> public-safe enablement hub that aligns with the public direction FedEx has
> already announced: AI literacy, responsible use, Dataworks-style logistics
> intelligence, and human-centered operational improvement."

## Strong Claims We Can Make

- FedEx has publicly announced an enterprise AI Education and Literacy program.
- FedEx publicly describes Dataworks as a data and intelligence layer for
  smarter supply chains.
- FedEx has public AI-enabled logistics products and partnerships.
- This repo uses public or synthetic data and keeps human review in the loop.
- The governance posture is consistent with lightweight risk management:
  define the use case, classify the data, document human review, and measure
  outcomes before scaling.

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not say this repo is an official FedEx product.
- Do not say the prototype is approved for production.
- Do not imply the app connects to internal FedEx data, dispatch, package,
  customer, employee, or route systems.
- Do not imply AI outputs can be used without manager verification.
