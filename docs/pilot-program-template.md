# AI Pilot Program Template

Use this template to propose a small, measurable AI pilot to your leadership or governance team. A good pilot is narrow, has clear success metrics, and uses only approved data.

## 5. Risk Review

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| AI invents facts | Medium | High | Human review of every output; source-checking step |
| Sensitive data leaks | Low | High | Synthetic/scrubbed data only; no real customer or employee data |
| Team resistance | Medium | Medium | Involve users in design; show time savings early |
| Tool becomes unavailable | Low | Medium | Document prompts so work can continue manually |
| Output quality is poor | Medium | Medium | Iterate prompts; measure against baseline |

## 7. Demo Readiness Checklist

Before showing this pilot to anyone outside the immediate team:

- [ ] Demo uses only synthetic or approved data
- [ ] Demo script is written and rehearsed
- [ ] Known limitations are stated up front
- [ ] No secrets, passwords, or private dashboards are visible
- [ ] Screenshots are scrubbed of sensitive data
- [ ] Live-action buttons are disabled or clearly labeled as simulated
- [ ] Human-review step is visible in the workflow

## 8. Post-Pilot Review

At the end of the pilot period, answer these questions:

1. Did the pilot meet its success criteria?
2. What worked better than expected?
3. What was harder than expected?
4. What would you change before scaling?
5. Is the data classification still correct?
6. Are the tools still approved for this use case?
7. What is the recommendation: expand, modify, or retire?
