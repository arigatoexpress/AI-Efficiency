# Leadership Brief: AI Efficiency Team

Use this one-page brief as the leave-behind for a regional standup, leadership
review, or pilot discussion.

## What this is

The AI Efficiency Team repo is a public-safe enablement hub for operations
managers. It contains copy-paste prompts, plain-English user guides, governance
checklists, pilot templates, and starter projects that show how AI can support
manager workflows without touching confidential FedEx data.

## Why now

FedEx has publicly announced enterprise AI literacy work and AI-enabled
logistics capabilities. The practical regional opportunity is to help managers
build safe habits now: remove sensitive data, use AI for drafts and analysis,
verify the output, and keep humans accountable for decisions.

## What the demo shows

- A prompt library for daily operations, safety, peak planning, reporting, and
  communication tasks.
- A public-data Logistics Intelligence System prototype for weather, road, and
  regional risk briefings.
- Governance templates that turn ideas into small pilots with clear data
  boundaries, success criteria, and human review.

## What it does not do

- It is not an official FedEx product or production system.
- It does not use package, customer, employee, route, facility-sensitive, or
  proprietary FedEx data.
- It does not make dispatch, safety, staffing, HR, legal, financial, or customer
  communication decisions.
- It does not send messages or trigger operational actions automatically.

## The leadership ask

1. Endorse safe experimentation with public or synthetic data.
2. Encourage managers to contribute real workflow prompts with sensitive details
   removed.
3. Identify one narrow pilot candidate with a clear owner, data classification,
   success metric, and human review path.
4. Require governance review before any internal data, production workflow, or
   outward-facing communication is introduced.
5. Keep internal data out until the project review checklist is complete.

## Suggested next 30 days

- Collect 10 manager-submitted prompts from real recurring workflows.
- Choose one low-risk pilot candidate that uses public, synthetic, or already
  approved data.
- Use the [pilot candidate shortlist](pilot-candidate-shortlist.md) to choose
  the first path.
- Complete the project review checklist and pilot template before any live test.
- Report back with time saved, quality improvements, risks found, and the
  decision to continue, change direction, or stop.

## Sources and governance anchors

- Use [presentation proof points](presentation-proof-points.md) for official
  public FedEx sources behind the AI literacy and Dataworks alignment.
- Use [project review checklist](governance/project-review-checklist.md) before
  presenting, sharing, piloting, or deploying any AI project.
- Use the NIST AI RMF lens in plain English: govern the use case, map risks,
  measure outcomes, and manage or stop the pilot if risk exceeds value.
