# CTO-Office Meeting Brief

## Purpose

Show that the regional AI efficiency team is serious, practical, and safe. The
message should be: operations managers can use AI to remove friction now, while
IT and AI governance retain the right controls around data, systems, and risk.

## Recommended Opening

We are building an operations-led AI efficiency hub. The first focus is not
replacing judgment or bypassing governance. The first focus is helping managers
turn routine work into reusable prompts, clean documentation, and small pilots
with clear value, clear data boundaries, and human review.

## What To Show

- This repository as the team knowledge base.
- The prompt library for non-technical managers.
- The Gemini and AI Studio guides.
- The project intake and governance checklist.
- The Delivery Markets Lab starter project as a prototype governance example:
  synthetic data, paper-only flow, no production API access, no live money, no
  customer payloads.

## Questions To Ask IT and Governance

- Which AI tools are currently approved for operations managers?
- What data classifications are allowed in each tool?
- What is the review path for a small internal pilot?
- Who approves Google AI Studio apps before broader sharing?
- What logging, retention, and access-control requirements should we follow?
- What does governance need from operations to evaluate use cases quickly?

## Proposed 30-Day Plan

1. Week 1: collect 10 practical manager use cases and classify the data needed.
2. Week 2: publish safe prompts and run manager feedback sessions.
3. Week 3: choose 2 low-risk pilots using synthetic or non-sensitive data.
4. Week 4: present measured time saved, risks found, and governance asks.

## Success Metrics

- Hours saved per manager per week.
- Reduction in time to draft briefs, handoffs, and training materials.
- Number of approved reusable prompts.
- Number of use cases rejected or redesigned because of data risk.
- Manager satisfaction and adoption.
- Governance review turnaround time.

## Clear Non-Claims

- This repo is not official enterprise policy.
- Starter projects are not approved production systems.
- No confidential FedEx data should be placed here.
- AI output is not final until reviewed by a human owner.
