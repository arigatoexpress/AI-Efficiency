# Pilot Candidate Shortlist

Use this shortlist after the presentation when leaders ask, "What should we try
first?" Each option is intentionally narrow, measurable, and safe to run with
public, synthetic, or scrubbed data.

## Selection Rule

Choose one pilot only. A good first pilot should:

- save manager time within 30 days;
- use public, synthetic, scrubbed, or already approved data;
- produce drafts, summaries, or checklists rather than decisions;
- have a named human owner who reviews every output;
- include a stop condition before it starts.

## Candidate 1: Shift Handoff Drafts

**Problem:** Shift handoffs are inconsistent and depend heavily on manager
writing time.

**AI assistance:** Turn scrubbed shift notes into a standard handoff draft with
open issues, owners, risks, and next steps.

**Data:** Scrubbed notes only. No package, customer, employee performance, or
route-manifest data.

**Success metric:** Reduce draft time by 25% while maintaining manager quality
score.

**Stop condition:** Stop if the draft repeatedly invents facts or misses safety
items.

## Candidate 2: Safety Huddle Prep

**Problem:** Safety huddles need timely, consistent messaging, especially during
weather, peak, or staffing changes.

**AI assistance:** Draft a five-minute huddle outline from public weather,
synthetic scenario notes, and approved safety reminders.

**Data:** Public weather and synthetic operational scenario notes.

**Success metric:** Managers can prepare a reviewed huddle brief in under 10
minutes.

**Stop condition:** Stop if the draft implies policy, procedure, or safety
direction that has not been verified.

## Candidate 3: Prompt Intake and Improvement

**Problem:** Good manager prompts are scattered and hard to reuse.

**AI assistance:** Collect proposed prompts, classify the workflow, identify
sensitive-data risks, and suggest safer wording.

**Data:** Prompt text only, with submitters instructed to remove sensitive data
before submission.

**Success metric:** Collect 10 reusable prompts with documented audience,
workflow, data classification, and review status.

**Stop condition:** Stop intake and scrub the submission if sensitive data is
included.

## Recommended First Pick

Start with **Shift Handoff Drafts** if the group wants a measurable manager-time
pilot. Start with **Prompt Intake and Improvement** if leadership wants the
lowest-risk enablement path before any workflow pilot.
