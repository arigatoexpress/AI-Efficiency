# AI Idea Intake Template

Use this template for safe AI idea submissions.

## Idea Title


## Problem

What task is slow, confusing, repetitive, or error-prone?


## Who It Helps

List roles, not individual names.


## Current Manual Process

Describe the process without sensitive data.


## Desired AI Help

What should AI draft, summarize, organize, explain, compare, or route?


## Data Needed

Describe data categories only. Do not paste real data.


## Sensitive Data Check

- [ ] No customer data.
- [ ] No package data.
- [ ] No employee records or performance reviews.
- [ ] No confidential screenshots.
- [ ] No pricing, contract, or bid details.
- [ ] No credentials or private links.

## Human Review Needed

Who should review before use?


## Expected Benefit

Estimate time saved, quality improvement, or risk reduction.


## Suggested Next Step

- [ ] Add prompt to library.
- [ ] Create starter project.
- [ ] Ask governance question.
- [ ] Build prototype with synthetic data.
- [ ] Reject or hold because data risk is too high.
