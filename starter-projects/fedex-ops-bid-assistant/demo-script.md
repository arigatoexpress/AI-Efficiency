# FedEx Ops Bid Assistant Demo Script

## Opening

This is a prototype for organizing opportunity or bid-review information. It
does not submit bids, set pricing, promise service levels, or replace official
review. The demo uses synthetic data only.

## Demo Flow

1. Open the AI Studio app or exported source.
2. Enter a synthetic opportunity scenario.
3. Ask the app to create:
   - opportunity summary;
   - missing-information list;
   - operational risk checklist;
   - review-owner list;
   - next-step plan.
4. Show where the output says "Needs verification."
5. Explain the human review path before any real use.

## Governance Talking Point

This is a good first AI efficiency workflow because it helps managers organize
thinking without allowing the AI to make a binding business decision.

## Close

The desired outcome is not automated bidding. The desired outcome is cleaner
intake, faster review, and better handoffs to the right decision makers.
