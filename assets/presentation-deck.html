The Daily Reality It Helps

AI is not abstract here. It removes friction from the work your managers already do every shift — at every station in your district.

Before the shift

Pull public weather and road signals, then draft a pre-shift huddle and safety note in minutes instead of scrolling five tabs.

During the shift

Turn a messy escalation into a clear handoff, summarize a metric spike, or rough out a customer service alert.

After the shift

Draft an after-action review, a near-miss report, or an executive update the manager edits and owns.

For a district: small daily time savings, multiplied across every manager and station, add up to hours of reclaimed capacity each week — without changing a single operational decision.

Prompt Library — 51 FedEx-Specific Prompts

Organized by what managers actually do, not by AI theory. A "prompt" is just a ready-made request your manager pastes into Gemini or ChatGPT and fills in the blanks:

  • Daily Operations — Shift briefs, handoffs, escalations, after-action reviews
  • Safety & Compliance — Pre-shift huddles, near-miss reports, seasonal alerts
  • Peak Season & Surge — Contingency plans, ISP coordination, sort hub staffing
  • Meeting & Communication — Agendas, action items, emails, executive updates
  • Customer & Contractor — Service alerts, escalation responses, team recognition
  • Linehaul & Routing — Feeder delays, alternate routing, yard management
  • Process Improvement — Root cause analysis, improvement proposals
  • Data & Reporting — Metrics summaries, trend explanations

Every prompt includes the Safe Prompt Rule: remove sensitive data first, review output before sharing, keep a human in charge.

The Efficiency Toolkit: Two Review Tools

After the week closes, two simple tools answer the two questions every efficiency review asks. Both open in a browser with no install and no internet — your real numbers never leave your machine.

Dock Efficiency Signal Lab — "Is the change real?"

Looks at your weekly dock numbers and flags which moves are a genuine trend versus normal week-to-week noise — so you chase what matters. Ranks best and worst facilities and shows the gap-to-goal in dollars.

TLH / SPH Explorer — "Which lever moved it?"

For any week-over-week efficiency change, it separates how much came from doing more per hour (SPH) versus simply cutting hours (TLH) — so a staffing cut is never mistaken for a real productivity gain.

Built for your view: filter by region or district (set up around the six Western focus districts) so you see your facilities, not the whole network. Demo data today; load a real weekly CSV locally during a pilot.

Appendix · Optional

We Test Before We Trust

When a flashier AI tool shows up, we don't just adopt it. We make it prove it beats the simple, explainable tool we already trust — in a fair head-to-head test — before it earns a pilot.

The first test we ran

A fancy AI forecasting model versus our simple, explainable one, on the same data. The simple tool won. So we kept it and spent no pilot, no budget, and no team time on the upgrade.

Why it matters to a district

This is how we keep hype from wasting your team's time and money. Nothing gets adopted on a sales pitch — only on measured results — and we write down exactly when it's worth a second look.

We also confirmed privacy-preserving (encrypted) scoring is feasible — kept on the research shelf pending a security review.
