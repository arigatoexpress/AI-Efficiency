# Safety and Compliance Prompts

FedEx's first value is **Safety Above All**. These prompts help managers draft safety-related communication without ever including real employee names, incident locations, or injury details in unapproved AI tools.

## Pre-Shift Safety Huddle Brief

```text
Act as a safety-focused FEC supervisor or manager preparing a 2-minute huddle brief.

Station: [Station name or code]
Shift: [Day/Evening/Night]
Season/Conditions: [e.g., winter ice, summer heat, rain]
Known equipment status: [e.g., all scanners functional, 2 dollies out for repair]

Return:
- 1 safety focus for this shift (e.g., slip hazards, heat awareness, lifting posture)
- 1 quick check everyone should do before starting
- 1 reminder about PPE or equipment inspection
- 1 "see something, say something" prompt
- End with: "Safety Above All. Questions before we start?"

Keep it under 150 words. Use direct, respectful language. Do not include real employee names or past incident details.
```

## Near-Miss Report Draft

```text
Help me draft a near-miss report from scrubbed notes. No real names, no exact dates if identifiable, no injury details.

What happened (scrubbed):
[Describe the near-miss in general terms]

Immediate action taken:
[General description]

Return a draft with:
- Category (slip/trip/fall, equipment contact, vehicle incident, ergonomic, other)
- General description of what occurred
- Contributing factors to consider
- Corrective actions to recommend
- Follow-up owner and timeline
- Note: "This is a draft for review. Official reporting must follow FedEx safety procedures."

Use neutral, blame-free language. Flag anything that needs verification.
```

## Safety Meeting Agenda

```text
Create a safety meeting agenda for an operations team.

Station/Hub: [Name]
Frequency: [Weekly / Monthly / Post-incident review]
Recent themes (scrubbed): [e.g., loading dock awareness, scanner strap usage]

Return:
- Meeting objective (1 sentence)
- 3 discussion topics (5 minutes each)
- 1 interactive element (quiz, scenario, or demonstration)
- Action item template with owner and due date
- Closing reminder about reporting channels

Keep total meeting time under 30 minutes.
```

## Seasonal Safety Alert Draft

```text
Draft a seasonal safety alert for operations team members.

Season/Hazard: [e.g., winter ice, summer heat index, high wind]
Affected areas: [General region, not specific facility details]
Duration: [General timeframe]

Return:
- Hazard summary (2 sentences)
- 3 specific precautions
- 1 "red flag" symptom or condition to watch for
- What to do if conditions worsen
- Where to find official FedEx safety updates (remind them to check internal sources, not rely on this draft)

Tone: calm, authoritative, caring. Under 200 words.
```

## Post-Incident Communication Draft (Manager-to-Team)

```text
Draft a brief team communication after a safety incident. No real names, no exact times, no medical details.

General context: [e.g., loading dock, P&D vehicle, sort aisle]
Key reminder: [e.g., stay alert near moving equipment, use three points of contact]

Return:
- Acknowledgment that safety is the top priority
- General reminder about the relevant procedure
- Encouragement to report hazards immediately
- Reference to official safety resources (internal)
- Closing that reinforces team care

Keep it human, not corporate-robot. Under 150 words.
```

## Safety Data Review Summary

```text
Summarize safety metrics for an operations review. Use only synthetic or public data. No real employee names or incident IDs.

Period: [Month/Quarter]
General trends: [e.g., slip incidents up slightly, vehicle incidents stable]
Focus areas: [e.g., dock operations, P&D backing procedures]

Return:
- 3 trends in plain English
- 1 area showing improvement
- 1 area needing attention
- 2 concrete actions with owners
- 1 metric to track next month

Separate confirmed facts from estimates. Flag anything needing verification.
```
