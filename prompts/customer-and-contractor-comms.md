# Customer and Contractor Communication Prompts

These prompts help draft professional, empathetic communication for customers and contractors. **Never include real customer names, tracking numbers, addresses, or delivery photos.**

## Service Alert Draft (Generic)

```text
Draft a generic service alert for customers in a affected area.

Area: [General region, e.g., "Denver metro area"]
Issue: [General description, e.g., "severe weather may cause delays"]
Expected impact: [General timeframe, e.g., "1–2 business days"]

Return:
- Brief, honest explanation (2 sentences)
- What customers should expect
- How to get updates (generic: "Track your shipment at fedex.com")
- Purple Promise tone: empathetic, professional, solution-oriented
- No specific delivery commitments or guarantees

Under 150 words. Do not include real tracking numbers, names, or addresses.
```

## Customer Escalation Response Draft

```text
Help me draft a response to a customer escalation. No real names, addresses, tracking numbers, or employee details.

Issue type: [e.g., missed delivery window, damaged package, incorrect address]
Customer sentiment: [e.g., frustrated, concerned, understanding]
Resolution offered: [General type, e.g., redelivery, claim process, manager callback]

Return:
- Acknowledgment of their concern
- Brief explanation (without blaming)
- Specific next step
- Timeline for follow-up
- Contact method for urgent updates

Tone: professional, empathetic, accountable. Under 200 words. Never reference real package details.
```

## Contractor Performance Conversation Notes

```text
Help me prepare notes for a performance conversation with an ISP or contractor. No real names or specific route details.

Topic: [e.g., on-time performance, safety observation, vehicle condition]
General context: [e.g., recent trend, customer feedback theme, inspection result]
Goal of conversation: [e.g., clarify expectations, offer support, document agreement]

Return:
- 2–3 objective observations (general terms)
- 1 specific expectation going forward
- 1 offer of support or resource
- 1 follow-up action and timeline
- Reminder: this is a draft for the manager to review and personalize

Tone: direct, supportive, fair. No real names, dates, or identifying details.
```

## Team Recognition Draft

```text
Draft a team recognition message for an operations group.

Achievement: [General description, e.g., "strong safety record this quarter", "successful peak launch"]
Team: [General group, e.g., "sort team", "P&D crew", "linehaul drivers"]

Return:
- Specific acknowledgment of the effort
- Impact on the operation (general terms)
- Thank-you that feels genuine, not corporate-generic
- Encouragement to keep the standard

Under 150 words. No real names unless this is for an approved internal channel where naming is permitted.
```

## Internal Stakeholder Update (Delay Notification)

```text
Draft an internal update about an operational delay for stakeholder awareness.

Issue: [General description, e.g., "linehaul delay due to road closure"]
Affected flow: [General, e.g., "westbound feeder schedule"]
Expected resolution: [General timeframe]

Return:
- Situation in 2 sentences
- Impact on downstream operations (general)
- Actions being taken (3 items)
- What stakeholders should do (if anything)
- Who to contact for updates (role, not name)

Under 200 words. No real customer data, no specific route manifests, no employee names.
```

## Delivery Exception Communication

```text
Draft a communication about a delivery exception for customer service reference.

Exception type: [e.g., address correction needed, access issue, weather hold, recipient not available]
General area: [Region]

Return:
- What happened (1 sentence)
- Why it happened (1 sentence, no blame)
- Next step for resolution
- Timeline for next attempt or update
- How customer can help (if applicable)

Under 120 words. No real names, addresses, or tracking numbers.
```
