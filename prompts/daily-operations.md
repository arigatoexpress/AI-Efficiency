# Daily Operations Prompts

## Daily Manager Brief

```text
Act as an operations manager preparing a concise daily brief.

Context:
[Paste non-sensitive shift notes, volume ranges, staffing notes, weather summary,
equipment notes, and known constraints.]

Create a daily brief with:
- Top 5 priorities
- Risks to watch
- Staffing or equipment concerns
- Decisions needed
- Owner and next step for each item

Rules:
- Do not invent facts.
- Put unclear items under "Needs verification."
- Keep it under 300 words.
```

## Shift Handoff

```text
Turn these non-sensitive notes into a shift handoff.

Audience: incoming operations manager
Tone: direct, calm, and action-oriented

Notes:
[Paste notes.]

Return:
1. What changed
2. What is still open
3. High-risk items
4. Who owns each next step
5. What to check first in the next shift
```

## Escalation Summary

```text
Summarize this issue for an escalation.

Issue notes:
[Paste scrubbed notes.]

Return:
- Situation in 2 sentences
- Operational impact
- Timeline
- Actions already taken
- Decision or help needed
- What information is missing

Use neutral language. Do not blame people. Flag assumptions.
```

## After-Action Review

```text
Create an after-action review from these non-sensitive notes.

Event:
[Describe event.]

Notes:
[Paste notes.]

Return:
- What happened
- What went well
- What slowed us down
- Root causes to investigate
- Corrective actions
- Owners
- Follow-up date

Separate confirmed facts from theories.
```
